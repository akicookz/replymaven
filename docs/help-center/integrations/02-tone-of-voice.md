Choose how the AI communicates with your visitors in project settings. Available presets:

| Tone | Style |
| --- | --- |
| Professional | Clear, polished, and business-appropriate. |
| Friendly | Warm and approachable, with a conversational feel. |
| Casual | Relaxed and informal, like chatting with a friend. |
| Formal | Structured and authoritative, suitable for enterprise. |
| Custom | Write your own system prompt for full control over the AI's personality (up to 2000 characters). |

The tone applies to every AI response in the widget. Company context, bot name, and the agent label combine with the tone so answers sound like your team.

> [!TIP]
> With a custom prompt, describe how to handle situations, not just style. For example: "When a visitor is frustrated, acknowledge it in one sentence before answering."
