---

Enable a "Leave a message" contact form on the widget's home screen. Visitors submit structured information when they prefer not to chat, and your team is notified.

Configure the form and its fields under **Support Chat → Chat Widget → Actions**.

- Submissions appear in your dashboard inbox.
- Telegram notifications fire for new submissions when Telegram is connected.
- You can open the form programmatically with `window.ReplyMaven.openInquiryForm()`. See [Open the contact form](/help/replymaven/widget-api/open-the-contact-form).
