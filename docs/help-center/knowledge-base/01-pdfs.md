---
title: Upload PDFs
slug: pdfs
excerpt: Use manuals, spec sheets, and internal docs as AI knowledge.
---

Upload PDF documents (up to 10MB each) as knowledge sources under **Knowledgebase → Sources**. The content is extracted, stored securely, and indexed for AI retrieval.

PDFs work well for:

- Product manuals and spec sheets
- Onboarding and setup guides
- Policy documents (returns, warranties, terms)

> [!INFO]
> Text-based PDFs index best. Scanned image-only PDFs cannot be read reliably; export a text version if you can.
