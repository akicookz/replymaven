---
title: Create FAQs
slug: faqs
excerpt: Structured question-and-answer pairs are the highest-quality knowledge source.
---

Create structured FAQ pairs (question + answer) under **Knowledgebase → Sources**. FAQs are particularly effective because the AI can match visitor questions to specific entries with high confidence.

> [!TIP]
> FAQs are the highest-quality knowledge source. Start with your most common customer questions for the best results.

Guidelines for good FAQ pairs:

- Write the question the way a customer would ask it, not the way your team names it.
- Keep one topic per pair. Two answers in one entry blur retrieval.
- Put concrete facts in the answer: numbers, limits, steps, links.

ReplyMaven can also draft FAQ-style canned responses automatically from closed conversations. Review and approve them in the dashboard to grow your knowledge from real support traffic.
