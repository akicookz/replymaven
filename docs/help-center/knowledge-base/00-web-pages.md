---
title: Add web pages
slug: web-pages
excerpt: Turn your existing website content into AI knowledge by entering a URL.
---

Add web pages as knowledge sources by entering a URL under **Knowledgebase → Sources**. ReplyMaven crawls the page, extracts the text content, and indexes it for AI retrieval.

- **Sub-pages can be auto-crawled** to build a comprehensive knowledge base from one starting URL.
- **Crawled content can be reviewed and edited** in the dashboard before and after indexing.
- **Individual pages can be re-crawled** to pick up changes when your site content updates.

> [!TIP]
> Start with the pages your customers actually ask about: pricing, getting started, troubleshooting, and policy pages. More targeted sources beat crawling everything.

See [How RAG works](/help/replymaven/knowledge-base/how-rag-works) for how indexed content is used in answers.
