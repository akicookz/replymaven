---
title: Real-time message delivery
slug: real-time-delivery
excerpt: AI answers stream in live, and agent replies appear without a refresh.
---

Messages are delivered in real time over a persistent connection:

- **AI responses stream** word by word as they are generated, so visitors start reading immediately.
- **Agent replies appear live.** When your team answers from the dashboard or Telegram, the message shows up in the visitor's open widget without a refresh.
- When the widget is closed or the tab is in the background, new replies raise an unread badge and, with permission, a desktop notification. See [Browser notifications and unread badges](/help/replymaven/conversations/browser-notifications).

There is nothing to configure. The connection is established when the visitor opens the widget and recovers automatically after network interruptions.
