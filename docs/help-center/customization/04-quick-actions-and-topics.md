## Quick actions

Quick actions are buttons on the widget's home screen. Each action has a label, an icon, and a predefined message to send. They help visitors get started without typing.

Configure them under **Support Chat → Chat Widget → Actions**. Use the sort order to control the display order.

## Quick topics

Quick topics are suggestion chips shown above the chat input. When a visitor clicks one, it sends a predefined prompt to the AI. Topics disappear after the first message is sent.

> [!TIP]
> Seed quick topics with your most common support questions. They teach visitors what the bot is good at and cut down typed misfires.
