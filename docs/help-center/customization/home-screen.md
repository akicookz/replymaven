# Home screen

The home screen is the first thing visitors see when they open the widget. It includes:

- **Banner image**: a header visual. Falls back to your brand color when not set.
- **Title and subtitle**: for example "How can we help?"
- **Ask box**: a quick input that transitions straight into the chat view.
- **Home links**: up to 5 links to external resources such as docs, blog, or a status page.
- **Leave a message**: a contact form button, when the contact form is enabled.

Configure the home screen under **Settings → Appearance**. Configure the contact form and quick actions under **Settings → Quick actions**.
