# Quick actions and quick topics

## Quick actions

Quick actions are buttons on the widget's home screen. Each has a label and an action type:

- **Prompt** sends a predefined message to chat.
- **Link** opens the configured URL.
- **Inquiry** opens the contact form.

Configure actions and their order under **Settings → Quick actions**.

## Quick topics

Quick topics are suggestion chips shown above the chat input. A click sends the configured prompt as a visitor message. The chips disappear after the first message is sent.

> [!TIP]
> Seed quick topics with your most common support questions. They teach visitors what the bot is good at and cut down typed misfires.
