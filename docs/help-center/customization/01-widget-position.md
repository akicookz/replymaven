---
title: Widget position
slug: widget-position
excerpt: Show the widget in the bottom-right or bottom-left corner.
---

The widget can sit in either bottom corner of the screen. Set it under **Support Chat → Chat Widget → Appearance**.

- **Bottom Right** (default): the standard position for most websites.
- **Bottom Left**: useful when other elements already occupy the bottom-right corner, such as a cookie banner or another floating button.

The position applies to both the trigger button and the opened chat window.
